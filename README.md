# GRC Portfolio

Governance, Risk, and Compliance work with a hands-on foundation. I design and implement security controls, then document them the way an auditor or a risk committee needs to see them: mapped to recognized frameworks, with honest residual-risk decisions.

I bring a 13-year background across digital governance, law enforcement, and organizational change, in media, healthcare, and government. My focus is the point where technical security, business governance, and adoption meet: risk that executives can actually see and decide on.
